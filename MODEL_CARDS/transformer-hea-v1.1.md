# Spectrum Transformer: HEA v1.1

Transformer encoder over normalized 4,001-point spectra. Train an independent
checkpoint for each split and seed 2027-2031; checkpoints cannot be reused
across split definitions. Report ID and all three OOD metrics plus matched
perturbation retention. The model does not establish unique atomic occupancy
recovery, and v1.1 has no experimental-noise claim.
